# Governance

## Stage 1: founder-led open specification (current)

- AgentCarousel maintains the specification. Every normative change goes through a public RFC
  ([rfcs/](rfcs/)).
- Maintainers merge an RFC only after a public comment period of at least 14 days.
  Implementers' objections are answered in writing in the RFC.
- The specification, schemas, conformance suite, and reference verifier stay openly licensed.
  Hosted or commercial AgentCarousel services never get exclusive access to a feature of the
  specification, and the specification never requires them.

## Stage 2: technical steering group

Trigger: at least two implementations not written by AgentCarousel.

- A steering group of five to seven people, with no more than two from any one organization,
  accepts or rejects RFCs.
- Members are drawn from implementers, enterprise security practitioners, independent researchers,
  and assessors.

## Stage 3: independent stewardship

Trigger: sustained multi-vendor adoption.

- The specification moves to an independent foundation or standards body. Its governance becomes
  separate from any commercial service.
